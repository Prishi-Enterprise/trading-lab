## Published costs checked 21 September 2026

Groww's [pricing page](https://groww.in/pricing) lists standard equity brokerage of the lower of ₹20 or 0.1% per executed order, with a ₹5 minimum. Delivery trading additionally has STT, stamp duty, exchange and SEBI charges, applicable IPFT, sell-side DP charges and GST. Its separate NRI and UPI Mandate balance schedules differ. Use the account's applicable schedule and contract notes before calculating actual trade costs; do not infer the applicable account category from residency or name. Tiny orders can have minimum/cap exceptions.

The journal currently accepts manually entered round-trip costs. It does not implement a Groww fee calculator. Include realistic slippage without double counting it if already reflected in fill prices. Contract-note amounts take precedence over a rough estimate.
